# Daki (Demon Slayer, Entertainment District arc): what makes her recognisable
Chosen form: AWAKENED (white hair with green tips). This is her battle look, the most iconic one, and the one the old game sprite used.
Other refs: 01 shows black hair (her disguise/early fight), and 04 shows her Oiran kimono (not used).

## Face
- Eyes: large and sharp, tilted UP at the outer corners. Yellow-GREEN irises with a dark slit pupil. A heavy lash line with
  RED eyeliner flicks at the outer corners.
- Brows: thin, high and arched, giving an arrogant look.
- Expression: a smug, cruel smirk with pink-red lips (angry and fanged when shouting).
- Marks: PINK FLOWER marks (a cherry-blossom shape), a big one on one cheek and one on the forehead. Pale skin.
- Earrings: dangling red ball earrings.

## Hair
- Silver-WHITE, swept up and back off the forehead in a high rounded top.
- Very long, falling in wavy locks past the waist.
- The ends turn into lime-GREEN wavy strands (around the hips).
- Hair ornaments: a crown of small dark-red/gold FAN-shaped kanzashi pins on top.

## Outfit
- A black halter top: two black bands crossing over the chest in a deep V.
- A black strip hanging down the front from the waist.
- A pink obi band around the midriff with an orange stripe.
- A crimson/magenta bikini bottom, with red ribbons tied at the hip.
- Thigh-high BLACK stockings with a magenta floral pattern and a WHITE band at the top.
- White ankle ties and very tall black GETA (platform clogs).

## Props: the obi sashes
- Huge flowing PINK sashes swirling around her.
- Pattern: a pink hexagon grid, ORANGE diagonal stripe bands, and dark four-petal flower motifs outlined in blue.

## Pose
- One hand on her hip, a confident wide stance, the other hand clawed.
