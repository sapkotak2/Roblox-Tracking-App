# Akainu (Sakazuki) - recognisability checklist (pre-timeskip look: 02 infobox, 10 face crop, 04 manga, 01 magma)

## Face
- [ ] Long, hard, square-jawed face; dark tan skin.
- [ ] Small dark eyes in deep shadow under the cap brim; heavy brow ridge with brows pulled DOWN into a permanent frown, deep vertical crease between the brows.
- [ ] Deep nasolabial lines from nose to mouth corners.
- [ ] Mouth: tight, hard, downturned line (or a clenched-teeth grimace when angry).
- [ ] Short black hair visible only at the sides under the cap.

## Cap
- [ ] WHITE Marine cap (not dark) with a short visor, two blue/navy stripes running over the crown, and a teal "MARINE" patch on the front (draw as a teal block, no text).

## Outfit
- [ ] Dark red / maroon double-breasted suit with gold buttons.
- [ ] Red Hawaiian shirt with ORANGE flowers, collar open and spread over the lapels.
- [ ] Purple tattoo on the left side of the neck/chest.
- [ ] Big pink rose on the right lapel.
- [ ] Black gloves.
- [ ] White Marine coat draped over the shoulders, long, grey-blue lining, red cuffs on the empty sleeves, gold buttons.
- [ ] Gold FRINGED epaulettes.
- [ ] Dark shoes.

## Power
- [ ] Magma fist: lumpy dark-red/orange mass with bright yellow hot spots, dripping, smoke/flame wisps.
