# Overhaul (Kai Chisaki): what makes him recognisable

## Face
- Eyes: narrow and half-lidded, cold and bored. A heavy upper lid line, small yellow-gold iris with a dark pupil, pale whites.
- Brows: thin, straight and short, sitting low and close to the eyes, angled slightly down toward the nose.
- Expression: flat and contemptuous. The mouth is never visible because the mask covers it.
- Ear: clearly visible at the side of the head, just above the mask strap.
- Mask: plague-doctor beak mask over the nose, mouth and chin. It is DARK RED/maroon with a GOLD rim, gold rivets, and a beak
  that curves forward and down past the chin. Dark straps run to the ears. (The black cloth mask is his civilian look, not this one.)

## Hair
- Short and messy, with spiky tufts on top and at the back. A few locks fall onto the forehead, but the forehead stays mostly clear.
- Very dark brown with a purple/maroon tint.

## Outfit
- A huge PURPLE fur collar wrapped around the shoulders and up to the jaw.
- A dark green (olive) bomber jacket, cropped at the waist, with a ribbed hem band and cuffs.
- A dark charcoal/black shirt with a light grey stripe or tie down the front.
- A brown belt with a gold buckle.
- Black slim trousers, slightly cropped above the ankle.
- WHITE sneakers.
- WHITE gloves (both hands).

## Pose / props
- The iconic pose has one arm stretched toward the enemy with a white-gloved hand open and the fingers spread (his quirk touch).
  The other hand hangs at his side.
