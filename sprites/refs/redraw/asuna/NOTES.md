# Asuna (SAO, Knights of the Blood) - likeness checklist
Refs: 02 (SAO avatar full body, front + side, colour), 00 (face close-up).

## Face
- [ ] Eyes: BIG and round (taller than Erza's), soft; thin upper lash, warm amber/hazel-brown iris with a light catchlight.
- [ ] Brows: thin, light, gently arched (not angled down).
- [ ] Expression: gentle, small soft smile.
- [ ] Face shape: rounder, softer cheeks, small chin.

## Hair
- [ ] Light orange-brown (chestnut), very long and straight - reaches the thighs at the back.
- [ ] Side-swept fringe, parted; long side locks in front of the ears hanging to the chest.
- [ ] Braid: a braid runs from the temple back along the side of the head to the back (crown braid), visible in profile.

## Outfit (KoB uniform)
- [ ] White sleeveless top with a high collar trimmed red; bare shoulders.
- [ ] Light grey chest plate over the white top.
- [ ] Red trim lines running down the front panel; white belt.
- [ ] Detached white sleeves (upper arm to wrist) with a red/gold band at the top.
- [ ] Red pleated skirt with a white front tabard flap marked with a small red cross.
- [ ] Long white coat tails hanging behind down to the knees, red trim.
- [ ] White thigh-highs with red cross/curl patterns; white boots.

## Props
- [ ] Lambent Light: slim silver rapier with a small guard, thrust forward; red/white sheath on the left hip.
- Build: slim, narrow shoulders, light fencer stance (different from Erza's broad armoured stance).
