# Izuku Midoriya (Deku) - recognisability checklist (refs: 04 2nd costume full body, 10 face crop, 02/03 gauntlets)
## Face
- [ ] Eyes: HUGE round eyes, big whites, small dark pupil with dark-green ring - #1 face feature
- [ ] Brows: short dark, raised (eager)
- [ ] FRECKLES on both cheeks (few dark-tan dots)
- [ ] Mouth: open eager grin/shout with upper teeth showing
## Hair
- [ ] Very dark green with black shading, messy curly mop, clumps poking out everywhere, bushy volume
## Costume (2nd costume, the standard hero suit)
- [ ] Teal-green jumpsuit
- [ ] Cream/off-white shoulder pads
- [ ] Grey metal respirator / mouthguard worn around the neck
- [ ] Black lines / panels on the sides and arms
- [ ] RED belt with pouches
- [ ] BLACK knee pads + black shin guards from knee to boot
- [ ] Big RED high-top boots with white soles
- [ ] Cream/white gloves with blue-white stripes at the wrist (support gauntlets)
## Effect
- [ ] Optional small green One For All lightning sparks around a fist
