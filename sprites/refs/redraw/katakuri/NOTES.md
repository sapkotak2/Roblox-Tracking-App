# Charlotte Katakuri - recognisability checklist (03 concept art, 00 infobox, 02 face, 05 manga colour)

## Face (top half only - scarf covers the mouth)
- [ ] Narrow, half-lidded, stern eyes; small pinkish-red iris; heavy brows that dip toward the nose.
- [ ] Bored/cold stare; frown crease between the brows. (Stitch scars on the cheeks are hidden by the scarf.)
- [ ] Tan skin.

## Hair
- [ ] SHORT spiky crew-cut, magenta/maroon, small spikes all around; NOT a big wild mane. Ears visible.

## Outfit
- [ ] Huge fur scarf wrapped up to the nose and spread over both shoulders: CREAM/white fur with thick BLACK horizontal stripes, jagged furry edges.
- [ ] Short black (very dark grey) sleeveless leather vest, open, with white studs along the bottom; spiked shoulder (manga).
- [ ] Bare muscular chest with a PINK vertical-stripe tattoo (pink/tan stripes) on his right side of the chest.
- [ ] Pink tattoo on his left shoulder/upper arm.
- [ ] Spiked wristbands (dark grey with silver spikes), dark brown gloves.
- [ ] Black belt with a white skull buckle.
- [ ] BLACK trousers; a studded grey strap wrapping one thigh; a pink-and-white knee pad with a spike on one knee; spiked bands on the boots; black boots.

## Prop
- [ ] Mogura: very long dark maroon-brown shaft with a gold collar and a black/silver trident head with three long curved prongs (outer prongs barbed outward). Usually held with the head DOWN by his side.
