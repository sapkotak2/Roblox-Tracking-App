# Kyojuro Rengoku - recognisability checklist (refs: 01 coloured body, 10 face crop, 03 tsuba, 02 sword)
## Face
- [ ] Eyes: BIG, round, wide open, staring; iris gold/yellow with a RED inner ring/pupil
- [ ] Brows: THICK BLACK forked brows (two-pointed at the outer end), angled up and out - #1 face feature
- [ ] Expression: confident wide smile / grin, mouth open or closed smile
- [ ] Face clean, no marks
## Hair
- [ ] Golden yellow, big and flame-like, LONG: spiky locks flowing back past the shoulders + side locks framing the face
- [ ] RED streaks / red tips (crown and lock tips); a little white/orange in the crown
## Outfit
- [ ] Black (brown-black) Demon Slayer uniform, high collar, gold buttons
- [ ] WHITE belt
- [ ] Baggy black hakama-style trousers gathered below the knee
- [ ] Leg wraps with FLAME pattern: red at top, yellow/orange flames rising (kyahan), dark straw sandals
- [ ] White haori over the shoulders (sleeves wide), lower half / hem covered in big crimson RED flames with
      orange-yellow inner flames; flames flare outward like wings
## Prop
- [ ] Katana: round FLAME tsuba (red/orange ring with flame tongues), WHITE-wrapped hilt, blade with red tint
