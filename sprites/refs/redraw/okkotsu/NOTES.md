# Yuta Okkotsu - recognisability checklist (refs: 00 JJK0 character sheet, 03 anime)
## Face
- [x] Big round eyes, white all around a small dark pupil (wide, empty stare)
- [x] Heavy dark circles / bags under both eyes (his trademark)
- [x] Thin, slightly raised neutral brows; small flat mouth; soft oval face
## Hair
- [x] Very dark teal-black, smooth and fairly short (NOT spiky)
- [x] Parted, with locks hanging down over the forehead (one lock in the middle down to the brows)
- [x] Sides cover the top of the ears; neat rounded silhouette
## Outfit
- [x] White Jujutsu High jacket, hip length, tall stiff collar, gold button on the collar
- [x] Diagonal (off-centre) closure line with a gold button; sleeves loose, cuffs pushed up a bit
- [x] BLACK slim trousers (drawstring at the waist)
- [x] White sneakers
## Props
- [x] Navy-black sword bag (katana case) slung on the back over one shoulder, grey strap across the chest
- [x] Katana drawn in hand when fighting (silver blade, dark hilt, small guard)
