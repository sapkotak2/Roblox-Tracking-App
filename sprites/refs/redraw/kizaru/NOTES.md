# Kizaru (Borsalino) - recognisability checklist (from 00 infobox, 02 concept art, 10 face crop)

## Face
- [ ] Long face, long thick neck (tan skin), long straight nose.
- [ ] Orange/amber-tinted sunglasses: wide, rounded-rectangle lenses with a thin gold rim; sleepy half-lidded eyes faintly visible through them.
- [ ] Brows mostly hidden behind/just above the glasses; forehead wrinkles (skip at 1x, maybe 1 shade line).
- [ ] Expression: relaxed, lazy open grin / half-smile showing upper teeth.
- [ ] Thin moustache and goatee stubble (dark), long sideburns running down to the jaw.

## Hair
- [ ] Short, dark (near-black) tight wavy/curly hair, close to the skull, not tall. Sideburns connect down the cheeks.

## Outfit
- [ ] Double-breasted suit: GOLD/ochre base with PALE YELLOW vertical stripes (two-tone yellow stripes, NOT white), brown buttons.
- [ ] Dark teal-green shirt (refs are clearly dark green, not purple) with a LAVENDER/purple tie.
- [ ] Matching striped trousers, long legs.
- [ ] WHITE shoes.
- [ ] White Marine coat worn as a cape over the shoulders: tall upturned collar behind the head, long (to the shins), dark blue-grey lining visible inside, gold buttons on the front edge, yellow cuffs on the empty sleeves.
- [ ] Pale-yellow FRINGED epaulettes (fringe strands hanging over the shoulder).

## Pose / props
- [ ] Lanky, relaxed slouch; hands usually in pockets. For the game: one hand up with a small yellow light glow on a fingertip.
