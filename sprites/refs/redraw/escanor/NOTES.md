# Escanor - recognisability checklist (refs: 04 Season 3 "The One" design, 10 face crop)
Chosen look: daytime "The One" (04): shirtless giant, the most iconic fighting look. Night form (01) and the
flaming Ultimate forms (02/03) are not used.

## Face
- [ ] Eyes: medium, slightly droopy/calm, BLUE iris, whites visible; not narrowed or angry
- [ ] Brows: thin, orange-brown, gently raised (arrogant-calm), NOT heavy black
- [ ] Nose: long straight nose, 2 px shade at the right edge
- [ ] MUSTACHE: big orange-brown handlebar, thick in the middle, ends curling UP past the cheeks - the #1 feature
- [ ] Mouth: small confident smile under the mustache; strong square jaw / chin
## Hair
- [ ] Ginger / orange-brown (NOT blond or ivory), short and neat
- [ ] Rounded cap, side-parted; fringe sweeps across the forehead toward his front and down past the temple
## Body / outfit
- [ ] Huge: very wide shoulders and arms, small head, thick neck, big pecs + 6-pack abs (shade lines)
- [ ] Shirtless, skin warm tan
- [ ] Brown leather belt with a small gold buckle
- [ ] Dark olive-green trousers (straight, long)
- [ ] Brown boots
- [ ] Pose: one arm raised holding Rhitta's handle near the head, other hand on the hip
## Prop: Rhitta
- [ ] Long GOLD handle resting across the shoulders behind the neck
- [ ] Blue grip where the hand holds it, a spiked gold ring at that end
- [ ] Axe head at the far end: gold crescent blade (horns up) with a silver edge, gold disc ornament + spear point
