# Chrollo Lucilfer (HxH 2011): what makes him recognisable

## Face
- Eyes: large, round and calm, with dark grey irises and a thin upper lid line. Never angry.
- Brows: thin and fairly straight, a little above the eyes.
- Expression: serene and composed, with a small neutral mouth (a faint smile at most).
- Forehead: a PURPLE ornate cross (a cross with diamond-shaped ends) in the middle of the big bare forehead.
- Ears: fully visible, with large round TEAL/turquoise ball earrings hanging below the lobes.

## Hair
- Black (blue-black), slicked straight back and tight to the skull, so the top of the head is small and round.
- The ends flare out in short spikes at the nape/back.
- The hairline is high and the forehead is fully open.

## Outfit
- A long dark PURPLE-black coat reaching mid-shin, worn open.
- A big spiky WHITE fur collar.
- WHITE fur cuffs on both sleeves.
- Gold buttons down both front edges, and a gold inverted cross on the back.
- CRIMSON coat lining, visible inside the open coat between the legs.
- A bare chest under the coat (lean abs), black trousers and a grey belt.
- Grey/white criss-cross leg wraps (X pattern) on the shins, and black boots with a gold mark on the toe.

## Props
- His book "Bandit's Secret", held in one hand, often open.
