# Armin Arlert (Survey Corps) - likeness checklist
Refs: 34 (official colour artwork, Survey Corps uniform + ODM gear + cloak), 10 (S1 anime face), 32 (official chibi).

## Face
- [ ] Eyes: very BIG and round, lots of white, large light sky-blue iris, small dark pupil.
- [ ] Brows: thin, often lifted at the inner ends (worried) or pulled down (determined, art 34).
- [ ] Expression: worried/determined; small mouth, slightly open or a short frown.
- [ ] Face shape: round, soft, boyish; small nose.

## Hair
- [ ] Blond (golden yellow in art, paler ash-blond in anime), BOB cut: round dome on top, straight sides covering the ears,
      ending at the jaw/chin with a slight flare.
- [ ] Straight-cut fringe down to the brows, slightly parted/uneven in the middle.

## Outfit (Survey Corps)
- [ ] Short tan/brown jacket cropped above the waist, with the Wings of Freedom badge (blue + white) on the chest.
- [ ] White shirt underneath.
- [ ] Brown leather harness straps: across the chest and around the thighs; brown belt; dark brown hip strap panel.
- [ ] White trousers; dark brown knee boots.
- [ ] Green cloak (optional, S1 iconic) - left off the idle sprite to keep the uniform readable.

## Props
- [ ] ODM gear: grey blade boxes on both hips (stacked blade segments), handle grips with triggers, long straight blades.
- Build: small, slim, shorter-feeling than Erza/Asuna; nervous-but-ready stance.
