# Erza Scarlet (Heart Kreuz) - likeness checklist
Refs: 10 (HK full body, colour), 11 (HK anime full body, holding sword), 00 (face close-up).

## Face
- [ ] Eyes: narrow, sharp almond shape, wider than tall; thick black upper lash line with a flick at the outer corner.
- [ ] Iris: very dark brown, almost black (reads dark, not the warm brown of Asuna).
- [ ] Brows: dark, straight, angled DOWN toward the nose (stern, determined).
- [ ] Expression: serious/stern; mouth a short flat line, no smile.
- [ ] Face shape: slim, longer, pointed chin (more mature than Asuna/Armin).

## Hair
- [ ] Scarlet red, long and straight: falls down the back past the waist, AND in front of the shoulders down to the chest.
- [ ] Bangs: heavy, parted near the centre, long locks fall between the eyes and over her right (far) eye side; in the
      close-up one eye is covered. Sprite: a long lock down the far side of the face, both eyes left readable.
- [ ] A few loose strands flaring out at the sides.

## Outfit (Heart Kreuz armour)
- [ ] Silver breastplate with a high collar/neck guard.
- [ ] GOLD cross on the chest (long vertical bar, offset to her left breast), small maroon heart mark at its top. (Not blue.)
- [ ] Big round silver pauldrons with horizontal groove bands.
- [ ] Black band/undersleeve at the elbow between pauldron and gauntlet; silver gauntlets.
- [ ] Silver plated faulds/tassets over the hips, brown leather belt + hanging brown straps.
- [ ] Navy blue pleated skirt.
- [ ] Bare knees; black knee-high boots with heels.

## Props
- [ ] Long straight broadsword: pale blue-white blade, light blue-grey crossguard and grip, held low/forward.
