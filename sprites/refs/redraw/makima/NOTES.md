# Makima (Chainsaw Man anime): what makes her recognisable

## Face
- Eyes: her signature. Fairly large and calm, half-lidded. Golden-yellow/amber irises with concentric RINGS (an orange ring
  around a yellow iris and a dark centre). A heavy upper lash line.
- Brows: thin and mostly hidden under the bangs.
- Expression: serene and faintly smiling. The mouth is a small closed curve, never open.
- Face: soft and slim, with a pointed chin. No marks.

## Hair
- Coral / salmon red-pink (anime colour: about (225,115,95)).
- Blunt, straight BANGS cut across at brow level.
- Two long, straight side locks framing the face, falling past the jaw down to the collarbone/chest, in front of the shoulders.
- The rest is pulled back into ONE thin LOW BRAID at the nape, hanging down her back to about the mid-back, with a small flared tip.

## Outfit
- A CREAM/off-white long-sleeved button-up shirt with a collar (not pure white), worn tucked in.
- A thin BLACK necktie.
- Very HIGH-waisted charcoal-black trousers with a wide waistband, long straight legs.
- BROWN leather shoes.

## Pose / props
- No props. Her iconic calm stance is standing straight with her hands clasped behind her back.
